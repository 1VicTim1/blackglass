# NULLTRACE seed data

`gov_domains.csv` is a small curated seed list, not a complete register. Each row links to the authority's public website. Matching a row records attribution and does not lower the risk score or prove that page content is legitimate.

`gov_prefixes.csv` intentionally contains no claims until an authoritative, redistributable source can be attached to each CIDR. Shared hosting or GeoIP location is not evidence of government ownership.

`gov_zones.csv` contains restricted government namespaces. A suffix match is displayed as namespace attribution, not proof that the specific content is authentic or uncompromised.

`brands.csv` is a manually curated subset of official brand domains with home-page sources. It is incomplete and must not be used as an allowlist or certificate/ownership verification. Similarity rules are offline heuristics.

Email MX, SPF and DMARC enrichment uses Cloudflare's documented DNS-over-HTTPS JSON endpoint only after user consent: https://developers.cloudflare.com/1.1.1.1/encryption/dns-over-https/make-api-requests/dns-json/

`hosting_prefixes.csv` is a dated snapshot generated by `scripts/update-network-data.py` from Cloudflare's published ranges at https://www.cloudflare.com/ips/, AWS's official feed at https://ip-ranges.amazonaws.com/ip-ranges.json, and Google Cloud's official feed at https://www.gstatic.com/ipranges/cloud.json. It is attribution, not a maliciousness verdict. Users may replace the packaged CSV with a sourced local catalog.

Online IP enrichment uses RIPE Stat `network-info` and its MaxMind GeoLite mirror after consent. Optional user-provided MaxMind/DB-IP MMDB files remain local. Country/city results are approximate network database records and may be stale or incorrect.
